# Twenty comparator desk profiles

Checked 30 October 2026. This is a purposive market map of direct and adjacent alternatives, **not twenty completed hands-on benchmarks or a ranked top twenty**. Closed, paid, account-based and native-app flows remain untested. No accounts, bank links or subscriptions were created. Popularity metrics have different denominators and provider provenance. None establishes effectiveness for the intended German preparation task.

The strongest adverse finding is substantial overlap: budgeting, reminders, files, exports, local encryption and debt inventory already exist. The candidate gap is the joined preparation workflow with dated/uncertain claims, collector relationships and a user-reviewed handover. Its demand, competitive uniqueness and adviser acceptance remain unproven.

## COMP-01 — Norta

[Norta](https://norta.app/funktionen) — Direct German debt-inventory/planning neighbour.

**Adoption:** No adoption count corroborated.
**Public documentation:** Inventory, plans, monthly budget and optional read-only bank connection. Public mapper tested separately.
**Decision:** Test its installed app before asserting a persistence or handover gap.
**Hands-on:** partial public web task. Does not count as a completed qualified benchmark.

## COMP-02 — Actual Budget

[Actual Budget](https://actualbudget.org/) — Adjacent local-first budgeting.

**Adoption:** Popularity not quantified in this audit.
**Public documentation:** Envelope budgeting, recurring costs, imports, undo and optional encrypted sync. Public demo annual-cost task saved and survived reload.
**Decision:** Do not rebuild a general envelope budgeter; preserve source/frequency clarity.
**Hands-on:** partial public web task. Does not count as a completed qualified benchmark.

## COMP-03 — Finanzguru

[Finanzguru](https://finanzguru.de/) — German multibanking/contract management.

**Adoption:** Company LinkedIn post claims over 3 million users; not independently audited.
**Public documentation:** Accounts, contracts, budgets, cancellation and AI money questions; provider screens and landing visual inspected, app not tested.
**Decision:** A bank-linked financial assistant is already a substantial adjacent category; avoid copying its AI/brand.
**Hands-on:** not performed; documentation only. Does not count as a completed qualified benchmark.

## COMP-04 — Outbank

[Outbank](https://outbankapp.com/) — German multibanking/local-data alternative.

**Adoption:** 4500 supported institutions is coverage, not user adoption.
**Public documentation:** Provider describes local encrypted finance data, accounts, budgets, contracts and household book; 14-day trial.
**Decision:** Local privacy is not novel by itself. Inspect actual exports and debt representation later.
**Hands-on:** not performed; documentation only. Does not count as a completed qualified benchmark.

## COMP-14 — Undebt.it

## COMP-16 — GnuCash

## COMP-18 — KMyMoney

[KMyMoney](https://kmymoney.org/) — Desktop open-source finance baseline.

**Adoption:** KDE community provenance; no adoption metric checked.
**Public documentation:** Cross-platform personal-finance manager with double-entry principles; ease-of-use and accuracy are stated goals, not measured outcomes here.

**Hands-on:** not performed; documentation only. Does not count as a completed qualified benchmark.

## COMP-19 — Money Manager Ex

[Money Manager Ex](https://moneymanagerex.org/moneymanagerex/) — Local files, budgets and attachments baseline.

**Adoption:** No adoption metric checked.
**Public documentation:** Manual documents describe budgets, forecasting, imports/exports, attachments and encrypted .emb versus unencrypted .mmb databases. Android security docs conflict by version.
**Decision:** App PIN is not database encryption; test version-specific recovery and data protection.
**Hands-on:** not performed; documentation only. Does not count as a completed qualified benchmark.

## COMP-20 — Firefly III

[Firefly III](https://github.com/firefly-iii/firefly-iii) — Self-hosted personal-finance alternative.

**Adoption:** Primary repository indexed at 24.8k stars; interest proxy, not users.
**Public documentation:** Self-hosted budgets, recurring transactions, rules, reports, double-entry and optional 2FA; technical deployment required.
**Decision:** Local/self-hosted finance already has strong options; low setup burden is a hypothesis to test.
**Hands-on:** not performed; documentation only. Does not count as a completed qualified benchmark.

## Next comparative tasks

For each qualified comparator: record version/market/tier/device; assess current adoption with explicit denominator; execute the same synthetic task; capture path, required accounts/data, errors, correction, save/reload, backup/import and reviewed export; inspect keyboard and narrow viewport; distinguish absence from inaccessible/untested. Recruit no one without separate outreach authority.

Synthetic task: one known 1200 EUR claim with original-creditor/collector identity; one unknown disputed claim; one possible duplicate; weekly groceries and annual insurance; missing document; manual follow-up date; recover a saved session; correct an entry; inspect a portable handover. Baselines include the free ISKA household form and Verbraucherzentrale creditor overview; forms are not counted as apps.
