# Maze Bank domain research protocol

30 October 2026. Initial candidate: German-first preparation of creditor, household and document information before a first debt-counselling appointment. The user authorises continued research and development while away; routine reversible work needs no renewed confirmation. No outreach, real financial records, payments, bank connections or actual lending are authorised by this protocol.

The product must earn its existence against counselling-provider forms and existing finance/debt tools. The 100-paper and 20-app requirements remain open until their records are complete. The shared foundation gives no automatic credit. Articles appearing in both corpora require a fresh product applicability judgment and count only once within the product corpus.

Questions: what preparation problem is consequential, what existing services already solve it, what users struggle with, what makes understandable/correct preparation possible, and what outcomes cannot credibly be promised? Separate the need for professional assistance and material income support from a software-organisational opportunity.

Search public primary publication sources and Europe PMC OA full text, retaining queries/dates. Full-text availability and index scope create bias. Exclude ecological/climate debt, sovereign/corporate finance, unrelated clinical studies, non-reviewed pieces and protocols without completed work from the counted evidence. Do not pad the count with medical financial-toxicity studies without direct applicability. Context-specific financial-behavior/capability work can inform hypotheses but cannot establish German demand or causality by association.

For each counted paper verify identity/review support; record design, actual sample/context, measures, findings, bias/precision limits, Germany/task transfer, product decision and reading depth. Full-text retrieval is not review. Related surveys/secondary papers and review overlap are evidence clusters, not independent replication. Preserve counterevidence and no-go reasons.

Benchmark 20 qualified relevant tools/apps; separate adoption evidence, provider claims, actual observed tasks and unknown/inaccessible features. Include the strongest free form-based workflow. No fabricated hands-on access or effectiveness/popularity rankings. Do not send messages or recruit participants without a direct instruction to contact them. Synthetic/agent task tests are not human usability or adviser acceptance.

Deliver justified scope, measurable acceptance criteria, comparative design rationale and honest readiness. If validation is unavailable, a bounded research prototype can support the comparative task study, but cannot be presented as a validated flagship or production service. Preserve original coursework and earlier rejected code. Do not bypass research gates or lower public claims to meet a deadline.
