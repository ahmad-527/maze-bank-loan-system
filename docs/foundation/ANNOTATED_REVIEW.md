# Annotated foundation review

Review date: 2026-10-30. Counted papers: **200**. Additional context appraisals: **18**.

Each entry is a focused critical appraisal of retrieved full-text passages, including methods/development, findings and limitations where relevant. This is not an exhaustive paragraph-by-paragraph/supplement review or independent replication. Publication identity is corroborated against cached DOI metadata. Review provenance uses an article statement where available; otherwise it is inferred from verified venue/publisher policy plus publication type, not from indexing alone. Individual confidential review reports were not audited.

This purposive, predominantly open-access health/digital-health corpus is not an exhaustive systematic review of all software research. Methodological and consensus papers inform process; they do not establish product effectiveness. Related studies must not be treated as independent replications. See the synthesis and protocol for limits.
