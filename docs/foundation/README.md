# Flagship requirements foundation

Completed scoped foundation review: 30 October 2026. The resulting governing document is [Portfolio flagship standard, version 2.0](../FLAGSHIP_STANDARD.md).

| Stage | Actual count |
|---|---:|
| Retrieved unique candidate metadata records | 1,061 |
| Selected full texts retrieved | 227 |
| Focused paper-specific full-text appraisals | 218 |
| Counted papers with publication/review support | **200** |
| Additional appraisals held outside the count | 18 |
| Counted papers with explicit article review statements | 28 |
| Counted papers using publication/type plus venue-policy support | 172 |
| Product-specific app benchmarks completed by this review | 0 |
| Human product validation completed by this review | 0 |

The 200 papers include empirical studies, reviews, measurement/methodological work and consensus guidance. They are not 200 independent experiments showing that a proposed app works. Each is counted once; related studies/guidelines still share evidence and cannot be double-weighted.

Reading depth was **focused appraisal of retrieved full-text methods/development, findings and limitations/discussion passages**. It was not exhaustive paragraph-by-paragraph/supplement review or independent replication. This is a purposive, open-access/Europe-PMC-heavy foundation with substantial digital-health representation, assessed by one AI-assisted reviewer. It is not an exhaustive systematic review of software or a substitute for finance-domain research. Peer-review support usually comes from publication identity/type and publisher policy, not inspection of every confidential review report; present policies do not independently prove historical compliance. No universal risk-of-bias score or pooled effect was calculated.

Use these files:

- [Synthesis and requirement implications](SYNTHESIS.md): what the evidence changes and where transfer stops.
- [Annotated paper reviews](ANNOTATED_REVIEW.md): findings and critical assessment for each of the 218 appraised records, with counting decisions.
- [Evidence matrix, JSON](EVIDENCE_MATRIX.json) / [CSV](EVIDENCE_MATRIX.csv): identifiers, full-text hashes, design, context, findings, limits, requirement mapping and publication checks.
- [Requirement-category evidence map](REQUIREMENT_EVIDENCE_MAP.json): counted paper IDs supporting each category, not a causal strength ranking.
- [Publication policy register](PUBLICATION_POLICY_REGISTER.json): primary policy sources and limits of review-status inference.
- [Protocol](REVIEW_PROTOCOL.md), [initial searches](SEARCH_LOG.json), [foundation searches](FOUNDATION_SEARCH_LOG.json), [retrieval log](RETRIEVAL_LOG.json) and [status](REVIEW_STATUS.json): audit trail.
- [Normative register](NORMATIVE_REGISTER.md): standards and provider guidance, outside the peer-reviewed count.

The scripts and cached source texts are private research working material. Do not publish the full-text corpus automatically. Publication rights for any later public research package require their own check.

The 100 relevant paper reviews and 20 qualified app benchmarks **for each individual flagship** remain independently required. This shared foundation does not establish any app's market demand, efficacy, security, accessibility conformance or production readiness.

This public dossier links to papers and supplies metadata/appraisals. Downloaded full-text XML and private review tooling are not redistributed.
